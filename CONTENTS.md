## Purpose

This index provides a short route through Chess-SSIL: a lightweight, independent research bridge from **Strategic Structural Intelligence and Competitive UIRTU-Relay (SSICR)** to reproducible game-based experiments, autonomous strategy-generation hypotheses, and collective structural learning.

The repository distinguishes established results in game-playing AI from **proposed** SSICR mappings, structural-growth mechanisms, and broader autonomy hypotheses. It does not require readers to accept the complete SI framework to test an individual claim.

## Minimal Contribution Route

Choose **one** hypothesis; implement or document **one** bounded experiment; compare against **one** appropriate baseline under a stated budget; preserve negative results and provenance; and publish a portable evidence record that another researcher can independently replay.

A successful contribution may be a confirmed improvement, a reproducible failure, a counterexample, a new evaluator, a compatible interface, or a demonstration that a proposed structural mechanism adds no value.

## Scientific Boundary

Game rules and evaluators can support autonomous search and learning, but success in a closed game does **not** establish open-world ASI. Structural evolution requires identifiable structural changes and fair, independent validation. Strategy-generation capability does not itself grant authority to act in real-world systems.

**Guiding principle:** *Build a verified generable core, preserve the residual frontier, and expand through evidence-backed structural relay.*
